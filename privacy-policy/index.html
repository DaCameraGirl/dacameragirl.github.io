ENV_OPEN_CLAW Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

ENV_OPEN_CLAW is a personal integration used to connect an authorized WHOOP account to an OpenClaw environment. This policy explains what data may be accessed and how it is handled.

Data accessed

With your authorization through WHOOP OAuth, ENV_OPEN_CLAW may request access to selected WHOOP data scopes, including recovery, cycles, sleep, workout, profile, and body measurement data. The app only accesses data allowed by the scopes you approve.

How data is used

WHOOP data is used only to display, analyze, or automate personal health and fitness workflows requested by the authorized user inside the OpenClaw environment.

Storage and sharing

  • ENV_OPEN_CLAW does not sell WHOOP data.
  • ENV_OPEN_CLAW does not share WHOOP data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • OAuth tokens and retrieved data are intended to be stored only in the user's controlled OpenClaw environment or connected infrastructure.
  • Data may be transmitted to WHOOP and the user's OpenClaw instance as needed to complete authorized requests.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as needed for the user's configured OpenClaw workflows. The user may revoke WHOOP access at any time through WHOOP account settings or by removing the integration credentials from the OpenClaw environment.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards should be used to protect OAuth credentials and retrieved data. Users are responsible for securing their OpenClaw instance, environment variables, logs, and any connected storage.
